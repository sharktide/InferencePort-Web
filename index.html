What it takes to run an AI application

For a support assistant that checks user messages, generates replies, stores conversation exports and tracks spend, compare the integrations your backend needs to maintain.

Example multi-provider stack

  1. Your applicationAuthenticate users and apply application logic.
  2. Amazon Bedrock GuardrailsApplyGuardrailModerate input; translate the provider verdict into your policy.
  3. OpenRouter/api/v1/chat/completionsChoose and call a model with separate credentials.
  4. Custom agent backendMaintain instructions, tool execution and model calls.
  5. Azure Blob StorageWrite approved conversation exports; manage storage credentials.
  6. Custom usage reportingReconcile provider bills and implement team-level limits.

With InferencePort

  1. Your applicationKeep your authentication, UI and business logic.
  2. AI ShieldPOST /ai-shield/scanUse content_moderation or prompt_guard; apply the returned decision.
  3. Unified model APIPOST /v1/chat/completionsUse a catalog model such as deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017.
  4. Managed agentsagent/<username>/support-botFor tool-enabled replies, configure instructions and MCP tools once and use the agent ID in place of a raw model ID.
  5. InferencePort BucketsStore exports using the workspace S3 endpoint and scoped SigV4 credentials.
  6. Organizations & usageTrack service spend and configure API key and model restrictions from one console.

Both are valid architectures. The InferencePort version reduces separate integrations and billing surfaces. Your backend still controls the sequence, enforces moderation decisions and chooses what to store; the S3 endpoint uses its own credentials.

Buckets Preview

Object storage that fits your model workflow.

Keep model weights, datasets and generated artifacts next to your AI infrastructure. Buckets brings S3-compatible storage to your personal or organization workspace, backed by Azure Blob Storage.

  • Bring familiar clients. Use AWS CLI or compatible SDKs with your workspace endpoint.
  • Scope credentials. Issue scoped, revocable SigV4 credentials and limit access by bucket, permission or object prefix.
  • Track storage separately. Monitor requests, transfer and storage charges rather than mixing them into model spend.

AI Shield

Protect your platform from fraud and AI abuse.

Shield brings account-risk analysis, content checks and AI-specific protections into one API. Supply the signals you have—such as an IP address, email, device or text—and receive a structured assessment with reasons your application can act on.

  • Sign-up and trial abuse. Detect bots, disposable identities, fake accounts and referral farming.
  • Identity and account risk. Investigate linked accounts, duplicates and suspected ban evasion.
  • Content moderation. Check spam, scams and potentially malicious messages.
  • Prompt and agent protection. Analyze prompt injection, jailbreak attempts and risky tool interactions.
  • Actionable decisions. Use risk scores, confidence and reasons to allow, challenge, review, rate-limit or block.
