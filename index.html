Desktop app

Private AI that runs on your own computer.

Download InferencePort, pick a model, and start chatting — no coding, no account needed. Local chats stay on your device.

  • Local chat, offline. Run open-source models through Ollama with streaming replies.
  • 45,000+ models. Download from the built-in marketplace or Hugging Face, or import your own GGUF or Modelfile.

  • Host a server. Share your models with teammates by email or API key, and connect to remote servers.
  • Spaces and sync. Open Hugging Face Spaces in-app; sign in to sync chats across devices.

Pay-to-Go API

One endpoint for every kind of AI.

Point your existing OpenAI SDK at InferencePort and call text, image, video, audio and 3D models. You pay per request from a prepaid credit wallet — separate from any chat subscription.

  • OpenAI-compatible. Same SDKs and tools; change the base URL and key.
  • Text with streaming and function calling, including Lightning models.
  • Image, video, audio and 3D generation through the same account.
  • Predictable spend. Buy credit packs; usage and cost are visible per model in the console.
  • Model catalog API. List models programmatically by type and capability.

Organizations

Give a whole team AI, with the controls IT expects.

Organizations live inside the console. Members share one credit wallet while admins set budgets, policies and security — billed per seat, free for up to 3 members.

  • Shared wallet and budgets. Per-member and per-key limits, spend caps and alerts.
  • Roles. Owner, admin, billing manager, auditor and member.
  • SSO and security. SAML single sign-on, enforced SSO, verified domains and IP allowlists.
  • Audit log. Exportable, with streaming to your SIEM on Team plans.
  • Desktop app policy. Choose cloud or local-only, which models can be pulled, tools, and who can host servers.

AI Shield

Know who you can trust, in one API call.

Send whatever you know about a sign-up, login or message. Shield returns a risk score, a confidence level, plain-English reasons and a recommended action in under a second.

  • Signup protection. Bots, fake accounts, trial farming and referral abuse.
  • Identity. Linked accounts, duplicates and ban evasion.
  • Content. Spam, scams and malicious messages.
  • Prompt protection. Injection, jailbreaks and tool or agent abuse in your AI product.
  • No rules to tune. One endpoint; send any mix of email, IP, phone, username, device or text.
