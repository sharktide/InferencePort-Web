02 — The bridges

What actually flows between them.

No hand-waving. Four concrete links connect the app, the console and your organization.

01

One identity everywhere

Sign in once with your InferencePort account. The same account opens the desktop app, web chat and the console, and it is the identity organizations invite, budget and audit.

App↔Chat↔Console
02

Keys and credits come from the console

You mint API keys and fund the Pay-to-Go wallet in the console. Your code calls the OpenAI-compatible endpoint with that key; each request debits the wallet. Lightning subscriptions are separate — they draw on plan quotas, not credits.

Console→ key →Your code→ debit →Wallet
03

Organization policy governs the app

Admins choose, in the console, whether members may use cloud models or stay local-only, which models can be pulled, whether custom tools and imports are allowed, and who may host a server. Members’ desktop apps pick the policy up within a few minutes or on restart. Lightning usage can bill to the organization instead of the member.

Org policy→ sync →Member apps
04

Spend and activity roll back up

API-key and playground usage billed to the org lands on one shared wallet, broken down by member, key and model, with budgets, alerts and an audit log you can export or stream to your SIEM.

Keys + playground→Org wallet→Usage · Audit
