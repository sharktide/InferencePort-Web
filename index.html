Buckets Preview

Object storage that fits your model workflow.

Keep model weights, datasets and generated artifacts next to your AI infrastructure. Buckets brings S3-compatible storage to your personal or organization workspace, backed by Azure Blob Storage.

  • Bring familiar clients. Use AWS CLI or compatible SDKs with your workspace endpoint.
  • Scope credentials. Issue scoped, revocable SigV4 credentials and limit access by bucket, permission or object prefix.
  • Track storage separately. Monitor requests, transfer and storage charges rather than mixing them into model spend.

AI Shield

Protect your platform from fraud and AI abuse.

Shield brings account-risk analysis, content checks and AI-specific protections into one API. Supply the signals you have—such as an IP address, email, device or text—and receive a structured assessment with reasons your application can act on.

  • Sign-up and trial abuse. Detect bots, disposable identities, fake accounts and referral farming.
  • Identity and account risk. Investigate linked accounts, duplicates and suspected ban evasion.
  • Content moderation. Check spam, scams and potentially malicious messages.
  • Prompt and agent protection. Analyze prompt injection, jailbreak attempts and risky tool interactions.
  • Actionable decisions. Use risk scores, confidence and reasons to allow, challenge, review, rate-limit or block.

Give any AI assistant a council of models.

One MCP tool call sends a difficult question to multiple models. They answer in parallel, critique one another and produce a synthesized result—without leaving the assistant you already use.

  • Works in your client. Use Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Desktop or your own MCP agent.
  • Control the review. Choose model and judge tiers, custom picks and 1–3 review rounds.
  • OAuth or API key. Authenticate securely; usage and review fees follow your workspace billing policies.

Lightning: chat and create in your browser.

Lightning is the ready-to-use chat experience—not the hosted model API. Talk to Lightning models, generate images and pick up conversations from your browser or desktop app, without configuring a developer environment.

  • Text and code. Use Lightning Text v2 for streaming answers, writing and development help.
  • Creative tools. Generate images with Lightning Image Turbo and access supported media tools.
  • One chat workspace. Start free, save conversations when signed in and upgrade for higher chat limits.

Building an app? Use the separate Pay-to-Go model API for programmatic requests and usage-based billing.
