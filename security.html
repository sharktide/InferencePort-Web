SECURITY

Security & Platform Safeguards

InferencePort LLC ("InferencePort," "we," "us," or "our") provides hosted artificial-intelligence services, developer APIs, security tooling, desktop software, and command-line tools. We implement service-specific controls intended to protect accounts, authorization, infrastructure, and customer information. NO NETWORK, AI MODEL, SOFTWARE, OR SECURITY DETECTION SYSTEM IS GUARANTEED TO BE ERROR-FREE OR COMPLETELY SECURE.

Security measures and responsibilities

Depending on the feature, safeguards include access-token and API-key verification, organization role and policy enforcement, rate limiting, request-size controls, selected encrypted data stores, abuse monitoring, and diagnostic redaction. We seek to avoid recording prompts and model-generated content in routine API logs. Certain administrative and usage events may include timestamps, actor identifiers, IP addresses, user agents, selected model identifiers, status, and resource or billing metadata. Safeguards differ between our cloud infrastructure, providers, desktop app, CLI, and customer-hosted systems.

Keep passwords, bearer tokens, API keys, local credential files, and tools confidential. The ipx CLI saves login sessions or API keys locally and restricts file permissions on supported systems; the files are not guaranteed to be encrypted at rest on your device. The desktop application may similarly store login state locally. The optional desktop and CLI local model proxies are operator-controlled; a hosted proxy may expose local services over a network. The CLI proxy does not implement the desktop proxy's optional encrypted-session handshake. Configure HTTPS/TLS or other appropriate transport protection, network restrictions, and explicit authorization if you enable remote access.

Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to security@inferenceport.ai. Provide a description and reproduction details without exposing other users' sensitive information or disrupting live services. Do not publish unremediated security reports that disclose another user's personal information.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy covers inferenceport.ai, the developer console, Lightning, Pay-to-Go APIs, model marketplace, AI Shield, managed agents, Council MCP, on-demand deployments, browser chat, desktop application, ipx CLI, organization features, and related services (collectively the "Services"). InferencePort LLC operates these Services. For an enterprise or school customer, we may process end-user data on behalf of that customer under its instructions and applicable written agreements.

Information processed and collected

Account and identity information: We process account IDs, email addresses, usernames, optional profile details, sign-in events, subscription status, and authorization metadata to provide accounts and access controls. Login is handled using authentication services, which may include email/password, supported OAuth identity providers, and organization SSO. We do not intentionally store users' plaintext passwords in our own application database.

Service and usage metadata: We record information required to authenticate, measure, bill, support, and secure the Services. This can include model or endpoint selected, token/usage counts, media generation units, realtime-audio duration, timestamps, statuses, quotas, credit transactions, reward ledger entries, invoices, payment references, deployment settings, key identifiers, organization memberships, audit events, security events, IP addresses, and user agents. Our payment processor receives the information necessary to take payment; our application need not collect full payment card numbers.

AI request content: An AI request necessarily requires us to receive, transmit, and temporarily process the submitted prompt, message, file, image, audio, video, structured decision data, embedding input, or AI Shield input and corresponding output. AS A STANDARD PRACTICE, INFERENCEPORT DOES NOT RETAIN RAW API REQUEST PROMPTS, INPUT FILE CONTENT, OR GENERATED OUTPUTS AS A PERSISTENT RECORD AFTER THE REQUEST IS COMPLETED, EXCEPT WHEN YOU ENABLE OR REQUEST A FEATURE THAT STORES THAT CONTENT, OR WHERE TEMPORARY PROCESSING, SECURITY REVIEW, OR LAW REQUIRES OTHERWISE. Normal service routing may use transient memory, short-lived buffering and caches during execution. Limited transient records such as hashed prompt-cache identifiers are not full prompt archives. We do not intentionally log full request bodies merely to bill an ordinary API call.

Explicitly stored features are different from transient API requests. If you save or synchronize a chat, use a hosted chat interface with saved history, store generated media, publish an agent, configure a managed agent with persistent instructions/system prompts or MCP credentials, upload a tool or file for retention, or use a feature requiring security/fraud memory, that feature may store its associated content or derived security indicators as necessary to operate. We do not describe those intentionally saved artifacts as zero-retention inference traffic. Public agent/profile/tool listings can be visible to other people depending on your chosen visibility controls.

Organization and device information: Organizations may retain membership, policy, usage, invoice, and audit records. Audit logs may contain an actor's email, IP address, user agent, event, and relevant non-prompt metadata, and may be sent to an organization-configured webhook. The desktop app may save local chats and configuration on your computer; ipx may save credentials, preferences, cached organization policies, and local server logs. These local files are controlled by the device operator, not remotely deleted by uninstalling the application.

Limited purpose, no sale and no model training

INFERENCEPORT DOES NOT SELL, RENT, OR BROKER PERSONAL INFORMATION OR CUSTOMER AI PROMPTS, INPUTS, OR OUTPUTS. INFERENCEPORT DOES NOT USE CUSTOMER PROMPTS, INPUTS, OUTPUTS, OR SAVED CHATS TO TRAIN OR FINE-TUNE OUR OWN AI MODELS. WE DO NOT USE CUSTOMER AI CONTENT FOR TARGETED ADVERTISING. We process information to provide the requested Service, route inference requests, enable explicitly requested persistence, administer accounts and organizations, bill usage, prevent fraud and abuse, handle support, protect our infrastructure, and comply with law. We may use operational statistics and aggregated or de-identified measurements that do not reveal the substantive content of your prompts to maintain and improve platform performance.

Cloud providers and subprocessors

Hosted requests may be sent to third-party model and infrastructure providers necessary to perform the requested task. The provider can depend on model choice and routing or fallback configuration. Best-Effort Upstream Privacy and Retention Exceptions

INFERENCEPORT SEEKS TO USE UPSTREAM AI PROVIDERS AND CONFIGURATIONS THAT DO NOT PERSISTENTLY RETAIN CUSTOMER PROMPTS OR OUTPUTS, DO NOT USE THAT CONTENT FOR TRAINING, AND PROCESS ONLY WHAT IS NECESSARY TO FULFILL REQUESTS, TO THE EXTENT THESE PROTECTIONS ARE SUPPORTED AND AVAILABLE TO US. We endeavor to use zero-data-retention settings or contractual protections where offered. A provider must necessarily receive request data to run its cloud model; therefore, we cannot promise that upstream providers receive or process "no data whatsoever." The intended limitation is on unnecessary retention, logging, model training and secondary use.

ZERO-DATA RETENTION CANNOT BE GUARANTEED FOR EVERY PROVIDER, MODEL, REGION, OR ROUTE. Some vendors require safety or abuse-monitoring retention, do not offer zero retention, or allow it only under express approval. For example, Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 generally requires 30-day data retention under Anthropic's model-specific policies, except where Anthropic expressly authorizes a zero-data-retention arrangement for eligible customers. See Anthropic's published retention documentation. This is an example of a provider-specific policy, not a representation that the model is always offered through InferencePort or that all models retain data for 30 days.

OUR OWN NO-SALE AND NO-TRAINING COMMITMENTS DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY EXTEND AS UNVERIFIED CONTRACTUAL PROMISES TO EVERY INDEPENDENT PROVIDER. Third parties may have different safety reviews, logging, retention, deletion, geography or training terms. We cannot override a provider's expressly stated retention requirement. Customers and schools requiring strict zero retention, no secondary use, or approved education-record processing must confirm an appropriate provider arrangement for their chosen route before submitting regulated or confidential content, or keep those workflows on their own local models.

Website information and cookies

Our sites may collect ordinary hosting/access logs and use cookies or local storage for login, preferences, security and session continuity. Site analytics may include GoatCounter; third-party identity, hosting, and payment services may process browser/network data under their policies. We do not sell this information or use submitted prompts for behavioral advertisements. Browser controls may affect nonessential tracking and can impair essential sign-in features.

Local-only operation and hosting

If you run a local AI model without signing in or invoking cloud integrations, model inference can stay on your device and need not send prompt content to InferencePort's hosted inference API. If you sign in, enable sync, invoke cloud generation, connect an external tool, download from a third-party model catalog, or expose a self-hosted server to remote users, that action can involve network processing and service-specific metadata. A remote server's operator is responsible for its own local logs, authorization and network security. We do not claim to control data held solely by third-party sites, independent model servers, or your own machine.

We do not set a blanket raw-inference-content retention period because we do not intentionally archive ordinary request prompts or outputs after processing. Transient buffers and short-lived caches are cleared according to system operation. By contrast, accounts, saved conversations/media, persistent agent settings, tool credentials, fraud intelligence, billing ledgers, security records and organization audit events remain for as long as needed to supply the enabled feature, protect legitimate interests, resolve disputes, comply with law, or meet an applicable agreement. Deleted information may persist temporarily in backups or systems managed by third parties in accordance with their schedules and legal requirements. We do not promise instantaneous removal from every independent upstream provider.

To access, correct, request a copy of, or request deletion of personal information held by InferencePort, contact privacy@inferenceport.ai. We may verify identity, honor applicable statutory timelines, and retain records required by law or active disputes. Deleting a local app does not cancel subscriptions, revoke issued keys, or delete cloud account data; deleting a cloud account cannot automatically erase your device files, independent providers' records, or school-controlled records.

Option 2 — Cloud Generation for the Classroom

With the necessary institutional approvals, an educator or school may use cloud generation for text, images, video, audio, 3D, embeddings, realtime voice/transcription, and typed decision models. EDUCATION RECORDS

Student Records — FERPA & Education Customers

Under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g and 34 C.F.R. Part 99, eligible schools and education agencies generally control disclosures of personally identifiable information from protected education records. A CONSUMER INFERENCEPORT ACCOUNT OR ORDINARY PUBLIC API KEY IS NOT, BY ITSELF, A FERPA DATA-PROCESSING AGREEMENT OR A GUARANTEE THAT EVERY THIRD-PARTY MODEL PROVIDER IS APPROVED FOR STUDENT EDUCATION RECORDS.

A SCHOOL-CONTROLLED LOCAL OLLAMA SERVER CAN SUPPORT CLASSROOM WORKFLOWS WITHOUT SENDING THE MODEL'S INFERENCE CONTENT TO INFERENCEPORT CLOUD ENDPOINTS. Authentication and authorization may still exchange user identity and verification metadata. For cloud use, schools can begin with generic educational material or non-identifying examples while reviewing providers and legal requirements. The school's ability to approve a service does not itself grant every third-party model permission to process protected education records.

Schools must independently identify a lawful basis for any disclosure, obtain required permissions, verify provider routing and student-data terms, and establish an appropriate written agreement and approved workflow before uploading or transmitting protected education records. Where a qualifying written agreement expressly designates InferencePort as a service provider or school official for an institutional function, the agreement must define permitted services and subprocessors, access and security requirements, the school's direct control over use and maintenance of records, restrictions on redisclosure, incident handling, and required return/deletion. Absent such an agreement and authorization, do not submit FERPA-protected records to ordinary cloud inference endpoints.

IF INFERENCEPORT RECEIVES FERPA-PROTECTED DATA UNDER AN APPLICABLE SCHOOL AGREEMENT, WE WILL PROCESS THAT DATA ONLY TO DELIVER THE CONTRACTED EDUCATIONAL SERVICE AND UNDER THE SCHOOL'S AUTHORIZED INSTRUCTIONS, NOT FOR MODEL TRAINING, ADVERTISING, SALE, UNRELATED PROFILE BUILDING, OR UNAUTHORIZED REDISCLOSURE. Access is limited to authorized purposes; disclosure to an approved provider is governed by applicable school instructions, contractual protections, and law. Student/parent requests about school-controlled education records should be directed to the school, which is responsible for determining access, amendment, consent and disclosure obligations.

FERPA's "school official" exception depends on conditions including the school's direct control and limits on use and redisclosure; this page does not claim that every deployment or school use automatically qualifies. Consult your school's legal/privacy office and the U.S. Department of Education Student Privacy Policy Office guidance before enabling external models or student-data integrations.
