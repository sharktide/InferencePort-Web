🧩
Anything else? Bring your own rules.
Every request can carry your policy: blocklists and allowlists (emails, email domains, domains, IPs and CIDR ranges, usernames, phones, countries, keywords, user agents, wallets, devices, account IDs) and up to 50 custom_rules — keywords or a regex, the fields to match, a category, a weight and an optional action. Shield fuses them with its own evidence, so a rule like "review anything mentioning Project Atlas" or "block sign-ups from these networks" works alongside everything above.
🎯
Accurate before the AI even runs
Deterministic detectors see through obfuscation (zero-width characters, homoglyphs, leetspeak, spaced letters, base64) and validate what they find: card numbers must pass Luhn, IBANs their checksum, keys their provider format. Independent weak signals add up; one strong signal is enough. The LLM reviewer then reads the redacted content to catch paraphrased attacks and talk down false alarms — but it can never overrule hard evidence like a leaked private key. A labeled test corpus guards every release: no benign sample may be reviewed or blocked, and no attack may be allowed.

API Reference

Five endpoints. Every cyber use case.

Base URL https://api.inferenceport.ai. Authenticate with Authorization: Bearer <access token or ipa_live_… API key>; calls to /check, /analyze, /scan and /detect-phishing count against your plan's daily Shield quota (/capabilities is free and needs no token). Pick the narrowest endpoint that fits, or /analyze when you want everything weighed together.

Check

One kind of indicator, one value or up to 500. The fastest way to answer a single question — is this email disposable? is this IP private? is this JWT unsigned? — with a verdict and typed facts per item.

  • type: email, domain, url, ip (or CIDR), phone, username, user_agent, wallet, jwt, hash, secret
  • value or values (max 500, de-duplicated)
  • Each result: verdict (clean / suspicious / malicious), risk_score, rules, reasons, facts
  • config.external_lookups toggles third-party reputation (default: only for ≤ 10 values)

Scan

Architecture

4 layers. 8 pipeline stages.

Every request flows through four architectural layers, passing through eight analysis stages. Each contributes evidence.

📥
Layer 1: Signal Collection
Accept any available information — email, username, phone, IP, device fingerprint, session metadata, request content, historical data, custom fields. Flexible schema handles structured JSON or plain text. Shield normalizes everything.
🔍
Layer 2: Intelligence Engines
Email Intelligence detects disposable providers and suspicious domains. IP Intelligence finds VPNs, datacenter IPs, and abuse networks. Device Intelligence spots reuse and automation. Phone Intelligence flags VOIP and temporary numbers. Behavioral Intelligence catches velocity anomalies.
🧠
Layer 3: AI Reasoning Engine
Specialized AI models analyze all signals simultaneously. Traditional systems evaluate signals independently — Shield evaluates context. Email alone = low risk. Phone alone = low risk. IP alone = low risk. Combined pattern = high confidence abuse. The AI reasons across all available evidence.
⚖️
Layer 4: Decision Engine
Produces the final risk score, confidence score, decision, detailed explanation, and recommended action. Supports allow, challenge, rate limit, review, and block. Every decision is auditable with full evidence trail.

Verdicts

5 decisions. Zero ambiguity.

Shield doesn't hand you a number and walk away. It tells you exactly what to do next.

allow
Request is safe. Proceed without any intervention. Zero friction for legitimate users.
challenge
Suspicious activity. Present a CAPTCHA, send a verification email, or require 2FA.
rate_limit
Exceeds safe thresholds. Throttle requests, add cooldowns, or cap usage.
review
Needs a human look. Flag for manual inspection before allowing or blocking.
block
Malicious. Deny entirely. Block the IP, ban the account, kill the session.
