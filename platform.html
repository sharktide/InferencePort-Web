Download

The place AI actually gets used.

The desktop app is a clean interface for local models, hosted models and the tools around them. It is local-first: chats and sessions are stored on your device, and local inference keeps prompts on your machine.

What you can do

  • Local chatRun open-source models through Ollama, fully offline. Unlimited and free on every plan.
  • Cloud modelsSwitch to Lightning (or other hosted models) for speed, image, video and audio generation, within your plan.
  • Host a serverShare the models on your machine with teammates or customers via accounts or custom API keys.
  • ToolsCreate custom tools, or install community tools from the registry and run them in chat.
  • ModelsBrowse and pull from the marketplace and Hugging Face; launch Spaces from inside the app.

How it connects

  • AccountSign in with your InferencePort account to unlock cloud features and sync.
  • Org policyIf you belong to an organization, its admins can limit cloud use, model pulls, imports, tools and server hosting. The app refreshes the policy within a few minutes or on restart.

Try Lightning

The fast path, with nothing to set up.

Lightning is the hosted chat experience: Lightning Text v2 streams up to 1,000 words per second and Lightning Image Turbo is the default image model. It runs on your subscription’s quotas, so there is no wallet to fund.

Lightning vs the API. Lightning subscriptions are tuned for everyday chatting and bounded by plan limits. For production services, use the Pay-to-Go API from the console — it bills a separate credit wallet instead of consuming plan quotas.

Open the console

The control room for everything you build.

The console is a web dashboard for the cloud side of InferencePort. It is where API keys are created, credits are bought, usage is measured and models are tried before they ship.

Build

  • API keysCreate and revoke keys for the OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
  • Pay-to-Go APIText, image, video, audio and 3D, billed per request from a credit wallet on its own ledger.
  • PlaygroundTry models and prompts before wiring them into code.
  • Subscription generationTry text, image, video and audio generation against your plan’s quota, signed in as yourself — no credits drawn.
  • ModelsBrowse the catalog of available models.
  • Agents & MCPCreate agents with a model, system prompt and MCP servers, and keep them private or publish them.
  • ShieldManage AI Shield, the one-call fraud and abuse decision API.

Account

  • BillingSubscriptions, credit packs and receipts.
  • UsagePer-model usage and cost, over time.
  • Authorized appsReview which third-party applications can act for your account.

Organization plans

One wallet. Real controls. A record of everything.

An organization turns individual accounts into a managed team. Members share one credit wallet, admins set budgets and policies, and every action is recorded. It reaches into the desktop app, so policy applies where people actually work.

Manage

  • Members & teamsInvite by email or link, assign roles, group people into teams, suspend instantly.
  • API keysOrg-owned keys with budgets, model and modality limits, rate limits, required expiry and IP allowlists.
  • BudgetsPer-member monthly credit budgets, per-key budgets and an org-wide monthly spend limit, with alerts.
  • PoliciesAllow-list the models and modalities the whole org can use.
  • Desktop appChoose cloud or local-only, control model pulls, imports, custom tools, server hosting and one-click server access, and whether Lightning bills the member or the org.
  • SecuritySAML single sign-on with just-in-time provisioning, enforced SSO, verified domains, IP allowlists.
  • Audit & usageSearchable audit log with CSV/JSON export and signed-webhook streaming to your SIEM. Usage by member, key and model.
  • BillingSeat-based plans, invoices, receipts and notification rules.

Typical setups

How teams actually combine them.

Most people start with the app and add surfaces as the work grows.

You are…You useThe flow
An individual who wants private AIDesktop appInstall, pull a model, chat locally. Add Lightning for speed when you want it.
Someone who wants to call hosted models on a plan, not creditsConsoleUse Generation (Subscription) in the console: it runs on your plan quota, while Pay-to-Go draws from the credit wallet.
A developer shipping a featureApp + ConsolePrototype in the app and playground, create a key in the console, fund the wallet, call /v1.
A startup with a shared budgetConsole + OrganizationCreate an org, invite the team, set per-member budgets, and issue org keys with limits.
A company rolling out AI to employeesAll threeEnforce SSO, restrict models and tools by policy, push desktop settings to every member, and stream audit to your SIEM.
A team hosting private modelsApp + OrganizationHost a server from the app; let the org fill the allowed-user list from your directory.

Pick your starting point.
