How billing works

Paid plans bill per seat, per month: Starter is $7.99 and Team is $20 for each seat.

  • Seats follow your member list automatically. When someone joins, the partial month for their seat is charged right away; when someone leaves, you get a prorated credit. Every renewal is exactly seats × price.
  • The Free plan covers up to 3 members.
