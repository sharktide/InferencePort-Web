02 — After you install

Your account connects the rest.

The app works without an account for local chat. Sign in with your InferencePort account to unlock cloud features — and the same sign-in opens the web console and any organization you belong to.

  • LocalPull a model from the marketplace and chat offline. Nothing leaves your machine.
  • CloudTurn on Lightning for hosted chat and generation within your plan.
  • TeamHost a server, or join an organization whose admins manage policy from the Developer Console.
